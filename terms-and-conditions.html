Anchor Reminders — SMS Terms and Conditions

Last updated: June 17, 2026

Program name and description

Anchor Reminders is a personal reminder program. It sends text-message reminders — such as tasks, important dates, and daily digests — that the account owner schedules for themselves. The program does not send marketing or promotional messages.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies depending on the reminders you create, up to approximately 10 messages per day.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply. These charges are set by your mobile carrier and are your responsibility.

How to opt out and get help

You can cancel at any time by texting STOP to the program number. After you send STOP, you will receive one confirmation message and no further messages will be sent.

For help, text HELP to the program number, or email colindaymude@gmail.com.

Support contact

For any questions or support, contact colindaymude@gmail.com.

Carrier disclaimer

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

Your information is handled in accordance with our Privacy Policy. We do not share or sell your information, and mobile information is never shared with third parties for marketing.
