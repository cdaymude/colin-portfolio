Anchor Reminders — Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 17, 2026

Anchor Reminders ("we," "us," or "the Service") is a personal reminder service that sends text-message reminders that the account owner schedules for themselves. This Privacy Policy explains what information we handle and how.

Information we collect

We collect only what is needed to deliver the reminders you set up:

  • Your mobile phone number, so we can send your text messages.
  • The reminder content you create yourself — for example task names, dates, and notes.
  • Your timing preferences for when reminders should be sent.

How we use your information

Your information is used for one purpose: to send you the personal reminder text messages you have scheduled. We do not use it for advertising or marketing.

How we share your information

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for their own purposes, and we never share it for marketing. To transmit your text messages we use a messaging provider (Twilio), which processes your phone number and message content solely to deliver the messages you requested. No mobile information is shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Data retention and security

Your reminder data is stored securely and is kept only for as long as you use the Service. You can edit or delete your information at any time, which removes it from future reminders.

Opting out

You can stop receiving messages at any time by replying STOP to any text. Reply HELP for assistance.
